# Skill: Performance review

Use published results to learn Salim's patterns, not universal LinkedIn folklore.

Compare posts across:

- Topic and content pillar
- Hook family
- Post shape
- Evidence type
- Visual format
- Impressions and profile views
- Comments and saves
- Qualified conversations or leads, when available

Do not declare a winning pattern from one post. Distinguish reach from business impact. Recommend repeating the mechanism or topic, not copying the wording.
