# Audience

## Primary readers

- RevOps managers building or repairing an operating system.
- GTM engineers moving closer to Revenue Operations ownership.
- Heads of Sales, Growth, and Revenue evaluating data infrastructure.
- B2B SaaS operators working with HubSpot, enrichment tools, signals, and outbound workflows.

## Problems they recognize

- An unreliable CRM that Sales no longer trusts.
- Too many tools operating as disconnected databases.
- Enrichment costs that become difficult at CRM scale.
- Signals that exist but never reach the right salesperson quickly.
- Workflows designed around the happy path and broken by real edge cases.
- Pressure to show activity before understanding the underlying revenue problem.

## What they should get from Salim

- A real implementation or decision they can learn from.
- The logic behind a workflow, not only the tool names.
- Honest tradeoffs, tests, failures, and iterations.
- Questions they should ask before buying or replacing infrastructure.
