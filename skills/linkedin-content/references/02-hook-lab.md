# Skill: Hook lab

Use for new posts and hook-specific requests.

Explore distinct directions before selecting an opening:

- A documented build: what Salim made or changed this week.
- A concrete result or constraint: a verified number with context.
- An operational observation: a pattern noticed while doing the work.
- A defensible opinion: a belief supported by experience.
- A story opening: a specific meeting, test, failure, or decision.

Reject openings that could introduce any generic business post, delay the subject, exaggerate the evidence, or use a tired reversal formula. The first two lines should create one open loop, not several disconnected promises.
