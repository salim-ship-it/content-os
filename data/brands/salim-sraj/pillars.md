# Content pillars

1. **Building RevOps in public**
   First weeks, stakeholder interviews, prioritization, CRM roadmaps, ownership, and lessons from moving from GTM engineering into RevOps.

2. **CRM architecture and data trust**
   Classification, enrichment, freshness, deduplication, lifecycle logic, routing, source-of-truth decisions, and Sales adoption.

3. **Signals turned into workflows**
   Champion moved, job changes, hiring signals, intent, qualification gates, edge cases, alerts, and assignment logic.

4. **GTM tool evaluation**
   Cost at scale, usage limits, orchestration, CRM synchronization, maintenance, agentic interfaces, and build-versus-buy decisions.

5. **Operating lessons**
   Working with founders, collecting feedback, enabling Sales, sequencing changes, and avoiding tool sprawl.

## Editorial balance

- Favor implementation stories and documented decisions.
- Use tool names when essential to the story, but make the lesson useful without the tool.
- Rotate formats. Not every post should be a numbered framework or a hook-body-takeaway template.
