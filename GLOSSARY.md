# Content OS glossary

- **Brand workspace:** The identity, voice, audience, pillars, evidence, guardrails, and performance data for one author.
- **Evidence library:** Approved facts that may be used publicly without asking for confirmation again.
- **Guardrail:** A publishing constraint that prevents disclosure, invention, or unwanted positioning.
- **Content skill:** A small, composable workflow stored under `skills/` and loaded only when relevant.
- **Creator reference:** Analysis of a creator's structural mechanisms. It is inspiration, not a voice to impersonate.
- **Source:** External or saved material used to discover ideas.
- **Draft:** Human or AI-assisted content not yet marked as published.
- **Content lineage:** The relationship between a source idea and its post, carousel, video, newsletter, or follow-up versions.
- **Performance loop:** Connecting a published asset to its format, hook, topic, and results so future recommendations use Salim's own evidence.
- **Runtime data:** User-specific state stored in Supabase rather than committed configuration.
- **Legacy documentation:** Retained historical instructions under `archive/legacy-docs/`; not the current source of truth.
