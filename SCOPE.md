# Content OS scope

Content OS helps Salim turn verified RevOps and GTM experience into high-quality LinkedIn content, learn from published performance, and reuse ideas across formats.

## In scope

- Brand identity, voice, audience, evidence, and publishing guardrails
- Idea extraction from user-supplied material
- LinkedIn hooks, posts, editing, and repurposing
- Creator structure analysis without copying creator language
- Post-performance tracking and pattern analysis
- Content sources, swipe files, previews, and lead magnets
- Authenticated, per-user application workflows

## Out of scope

- Automatic publication without explicit user action
- Invented experiences, metrics, client results, or opinions
- Storage of secrets in committed files
- Treating scraped creator content as Salim's original material
- Client work as application configuration
- Generated media and exports in the repository root

## Current default

The active brand is Salim Sraj. `CONTENT_OS_BRAND` may select another workspace later, but additional brands must remain isolated under `data/brands/<slug>/`.
