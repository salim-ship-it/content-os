# The Sandcastles Reel Idea Workflow

**What this is:** the exact process for turning Sandcastles.ai into a founder-content-idea machine — from "empty feed" to a scripted Reel in your own voice.

---

## STEP 1 — Set Up Your Feed

Don't track random big accounts. Track accounts that match your ICP on two things:

- **Same niche** — other founders, coaches, or consultants talking to the same audience you are
- **Similar range** — not just the 1M+ mega accounts. Include a few in the 5K-50K range. Their wins are easier to repeat than a mega-viral outlier.

Add 8-12 channels. Fewer and you run out of source material. More and you never actually watch anything.

---

## STEP 2 — The Viral Breakdown Checklist

For every top-performing video Sandcastles surfaces, answer these 5 questions before you touch a script:

1. **What's the hook, word for word, in the first 2 seconds?**
2. **What's the pattern interrupt?** (a visual cut, a stat, a contrarian line — the thing that stops the scroll after the hook)
3. **What's the actual promise?** What does the viewer think they'll get if they keep watching?
4. **What's the format?** Talking head, voiceover + b-roll, text-on-screen, carousel-to-reel.
5. **Does it pay off the hook?** Does the video actually deliver what line 1 promised, or does it bait and switch?

Log the answers. This is the part most people skip — they screenshot the hook and skip straight to copying it, which is why their version flops.

---

## STEP 3 — Hook Templates (Pull, Don't Copy)

Once you've logged 15-20 breakdowns, you'll see the same hook shapes repeating. The main ones:

- **Stat/data hook** — "50% of creators miss this"
- **Contrarian hook** — "Everyone tells you to post daily. Here's why that's wrong."
- **Direct question hook** — "Why do some accounts hit 10K in a month?"
- **Story hook** — "A stranger gave me back what I'd lost."

Take the *shape*, not the words. Swap in your own number, your own belief, your own story.

---

## STEP 4 — Transcript-to-Script Prompt

Pull the transcript of a video whose shape you want to reuse (Sandcastles does this in one click), then run this:

> You are a short-form scriptwriter. Below is a transcript of a video that performed well. Identify the structural shape only — hook, pattern interrupt, promise, payoff, CTA. Do not reuse any of the original wording, examples, or claims. Then write a new script in that same shape using the following facts about me: [paste your question/mistake/result/belief — see the Content Hook Bank framework]. The result should sound nothing like the source video in content, only in structure.

---

## END OF WORKFLOW

Run steps 1-4 once a week. You'll walk away with a stack of scripts that are structurally proven but 100% your own voice — no idea, wording, or claim borrowed from anyone else.
