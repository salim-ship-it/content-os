# Moataz — Post Format Frameworks

## Format 1: The Results Post

**Structure:**
1. Hook with specific result
2. Context: What most people do wrong
3. The different approach
4. Why it works (1-2 key reasons)
5. CTA (DM "Grow", Book call)

**Example Opening:**
"Our latest client went from 7K to 200K followers in 7 months. Here's how we did it..."

**Length:** 300-400 words

---

## Format 2: The Contrarian Take

**Structure:**
1. Statement that contradicts common belief
2. Why people believe the wrong thing
3. What's actually true (backed by data)
4. What to do instead
5. CTA

**Example Opening:**
"Everyone says you need to buy ads to grow your audience. They're wrong. Here's why..."

**Length:** 250-350 words

---

## Format 3: The Question Post

**Structure:**
1. Provocative question
2. Why most people get it wrong
3. The real answer
4. Actionable next step
5. CTA (DM answer or book call)

**Example Opening:**
"How do you turn 50K followers into $500K in revenue?"

**Length:** 200-300 words

---

## Format 4: The Process Post

**Structure:**
1. Hook: "Here's our exact process for X"
2. Step 1 with detail
3. Step 2 with detail
4. Step 3 with detail (optional)
5. The outcome
6. CTA: "This is why we only take 3 clients per quarter"

**Example Opening:**
"Here's the exact content engine we build for every client..."

**Length:** 350-450 words

---

## Format 5: The Mistake Post

**Structure:**
1. Mistake most creators make (specific)
2. Why it's a mistake (psychology/results)
3. How to fix it
4. Result when done right
5. CTA

**Example Opening:**
"The #1 mistake creators make when monetizing..."

**Length:** 250-350 words

---

## Format 6: The Scarcity Post

**Structure:**
1. Statement about limited availability
2. Why we keep it small
3. What happens when you work with us
4. Who should apply
5. Hard CTA (book call, limited spots)

**Example Opening:**
"Only 3 seats open for Q3. Here's who we take..."

**Length:** 150-250 words

---

## Universal Rules for All Formats

### Opening Requirements
- Always hook within first 2 lines
- Make reader want to know the answer
- Use specific numbers/results when possible

### Middle Requirements
- Break into short paragraphs (1-3 sentences max)
- One key insight per paragraph
- Support claims with data when possible

### Closing Requirements
- Clear CTA (never vague)
- Options: DM "Grow", Book call, Apply link
- Reinforce scarcity if applicable

### Tone Requirements
- Conversational (imagine talking to someone)
- Confident but not arrogant
- Action-oriented
- No fluff or filler

### What NOT to Do
- Don't use generic motivational language
- Don't go more than 2-3 sentences per paragraph
- Don't forget social proof (numbers, clients, results)
- Don't make it about theory, make it about results
- Don't use complicated words

## Content Calendar Suggestion

**Weekly Mix (5 posts):**
- 1x Results Post (case study)
- 1x Process Post (how-to)
- 1x Contrarian Take (opinion)
- 1x Question Post (engagement)
- 1x Mistake Post (lesson learned)

**Monthly Mix:**
- 1-2 Scarcity Posts (limited seats)
- 1 Deep dive (2-3 posts on same topic)
- 1 Collaboration mention
- Mix of educational + promotional

---

## Key Metrics to Track

For each post, measure:
- Engagement rate (likes + comments / impressions)
- Comment sentiment (are people engaged?)
- DM responses to CTAs
- Booking rates from posts

Best performing formats get repeated more often.
