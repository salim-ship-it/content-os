At 22 I left home to build a life somewhere else.

It took a few years to realise I'd left it permanently.

Not the country. The feeling.


Here, I'm the foreigner. The accent. The paperwork.

There, I'm the visitor. The one who missed things.


Nobody warns you about this part.


You don't lose one home when you move.

You slowly stop having one.


The friends back home built lives you're not in.

The friends here met you fully formed, so they only know half of you.

Your family updates you like a newsletter.

And you update them the same way.


For a long time I thought something was wrong with me.


It isn't. It's just the price nobody prints on the ticket.


Here's what I've made peace with:


Home stopped being a place I go back to.

It became the people who still answer at 2am, in two time zones.


To anyone living between two countries right now:


You're not ungrateful. You're not lost.

You're carrying two lives, and neither one is full-time.


That's allowed.


Love, someone who still doesn't know where to spend Christmas ♥️
